# GSE3183 data audit

Audit date: 2026-09-06

## Decision

GSE3183 is technically ingestible but is only conditionally suitable as the biological anchor for Phase 1.

The deposited data contain a complete set of 15 raw CEL files and unambiguous treatment/time labels. However, the samples are from the A549 lung adenocarcinoma cell line, not primary human airway epithelium. The design is also incomplete: untreated controls are available at 0 and 24 hours, whereas IL-13-treated samples are available at 4, 12, and 24 hours. Consequently, intermediate treatment effects cannot be separated cleanly from time-dependent culture effects.

Use GSE3183 as an early-response feasibility and engineering dataset. Do not use it alone to claim identifiable primary-airway signaling dynamics or direct transfer of kinetic parameters to GSE240741.

## Experimental design

| Condition | 0 h | 4 h | 12 h | 24 h | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Control | 3 | 0 | 0 | 3 | 6 |
| IL-13 | 0 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 9 |
| Total | 3 | 3 | 3 | 6 | 15 |

The full accession-level mapping is tracked in [`data/metadata/GSE3183_samples.csv`](../../data/metadata/GSE3183_samples.csv).

The title suffixes `a`, `b`, and `c` define three replicate labels within each observed group. GEO does not state whether these are independent biological cultures, technical replicates, or another replication level. They are therefore recorded as `replicate_type=undocumented`, not as biological replicates.

## Normalization status

The deposited SOFT and Series Matrix expose:

- `VALUE`: labelled only as “SIGNAL (See Affymetrix Literature)”
- `ABS_CALL`: Affymetrix present/absent transcript call

The exact background correction, normalization, summarization algorithm, software version, and batch-processing scope are not documented in the deposited metadata. The numerical values are on a linear-looking signal scale, but identifying them as MAS5 or another Affymetrix procedure would be inference.

For the reproducible workflow, use the raw CEL archive as the authoritative expression input and define normalization explicitly. The deposited matrix can be retained for cross-checking, not silently treated as a reproducible normalized matrix.

## Missing metadata

The following fields could not be recovered from GEO:

- IL-13 concentration
- treatment vehicle
- culture and stimulation protocol
- replicate type and independence
- passage, batch, sex, donor, or provenance of the A549 cells
- a primary publication directly describing the experiment

These fields must remain missing in the data model. They must not be filled from a different IL-13/A549 publication without evidence that it describes GSE3183.

## Consequences for Phase 1

1. Treat the three arrays per group as assay replicates of undocumented type.
2. Do not describe GSE3183 as primary airway epithelial data.
3. Estimate the 24-hour IL-13 effect against the matched 24-hour control.
4. Treat the 4-hour and 12-hour contrasts against 0 hours as a mixture of treatment response and possible time-in-culture drift.
5. Restrict initial dynamics to exploratory module trajectories and simple baselines.
6. Add a dataset-suitability gate before interpreting latent states or transferring kinetic parameters to Phase 2.
